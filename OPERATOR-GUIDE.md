# GOGO Fleet — Operator Quick Start

Welcome! About 20 minutes to your first rental.

## 1. Create your account
Go to **gogofleet.app/signup.html**. No card needed; you get a 14-day free trial.
Install it to your phone: open the site, Share → **Add to Home Screen**.

## 2. Finish your profile
**Team → Business Profile**: enter your business name and phone. Renters see these on their portal and contract.

## 3. Add your cars
**Fleet → + Add Vehicle**: year, make, model, plate, VIN, purchase price. (Starter plan: up to 5 cars.)

## 4. Set up payouts (so renters can pay by card)
**Team → Plan & Billing → Set up payouts.** Stripe asks for ID and bank details (~5 min). Rent then goes straight to your bank. A card processing fee of about 2.9% + 30¢ applies per payment. Until this is done, renters are told to pay you directly and you can still record payments by hand.

## 5. Put a renter in a car
**Fleet → the car → Assign Renter**: name, phone, driver's license, weekly rent, protection fee, deposit, dates. Tick the SMS-consent box (renter agreed to texts).
The renter gets a text with the link **gogofleet.app/renter.html** and a 4-digit PIN.

## 6. What the renter does
1. Opens the link, enters their phone number and PIN.
2. Reads and **signs the rental agreement** with their finger. It names your business, and once signed it can't be changed.
3. Uploads their driver's license and insurance.
4. Pays each week with **Pay Now** (if payouts are set up).
You see the signed contract and documents on the car's page.

## 7. Choose a plan before the trial ends
**Team → Plan & Billing**: Starter $49/mo (5 cars), Growth $149/mo (15), Scale $279/mo (unlimited). After the trial you can keep everything but can't add new cars until you subscribe.

## Texas checklist (not legal advice)
Have a Texas attorney review your rental agreement. Confirm with your insurance agent that the cars are covered for rideshare/delivery use. Check city and state rules on vehicle rentals and sales tax, and keep deposit and payment records.
